# 04 · Resource Plan (RACI chart and Resource Allocation Overview): writing guide

**Owner suggestion:** Member 2. **Length:** 2 to 3 pages. **Time:** 25 to 35 minutes.

## Purpose

Show **who did what** (RACI), **what resources the project used** and how the work was shared (allocation). The brief asks for "Resource Plan (RACI Chart, Resource Allocation)".

## Key ideas explained simply

**RACI chart:** a table with activities down the side and people across the top. Each cell holds one letter:
- **R, Responsible:** does the work.
- **A, Accountable:** owns the result and signs it off. **Exactly one A per row.**
- **C, Consulted:** gives input before the work is finished (two-way).
- **I, Informed:** told about progress or results (one-way).
A person can be both A and R. Every activity needs at least one R and exactly one A.

**Resource allocation:** all resources (people, tools, infrastructure, money, time) and how they were used or divided.

## Step 4: short paragraphs (your words)

1. **How the work was divided and why** (strengths, interests, availability).
2. **Resource constraints you faced** (team of three instead of the brief's suggested six roles, limited time, no budget) and how you handled them.
3. **Workload balancing:** did anyone become a bottleneck? What did you change?
4. **Future resource needs** if the project continued (hosting, email, payment gateway fees, support person).

## Common mistakes

- More than one A (or no A) in a row.
- A RACI that does not match what really happened or the video.
- Listing six roles but only three people, with no explanation.
- Inventing costs or hours. Use real or clearly labelled estimates.
